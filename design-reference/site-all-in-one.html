What's BEHYBRID?
BEHYBRID is a group of individuals pushing their limits, at their own level, toward their own goals. Different paces, same spirit: run, improve and enjoy. BEHYBRID, it's you, them and us.
Community
Connect with runners who push limits and never settle.
Weekly Events
Perform & Connect runs every week, rain or shine.
Training
Programs built for runners and hybrid athletes.
For All Paces
Everyone is welcome. No ego. Just the love of running.
